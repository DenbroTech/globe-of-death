# Globe Riders (formerly Globe of Death) — project handover

A phone-first 3D rhythm stunt game. You ride a motorbike inside a steel "globe of death" cage, hitting moves to the beat while a crowd watches. Built with three.js r128 + Vite, ES modules in `src/`. It started life as a single-file claude.ai artifact (kept in `original/globe-of-death.html` for comparison) and ships as an Android app: Expo shell in `mobile/` (WebView showing the single-file build `mobile/assets/game.html`), built in the cloud with EAS like the owner's Flippy Mat game.

## The owner (how to work with them)
- Tests on an Android phone (Android only for now), sometimes on desktop. Builds the app on their Windows PC with `npx eas-cli build --platform android --profile preview` (same workflow as their Flippy Mat game). Phone-first always: portrait, thumb controls, 60 fps target.
- Wants things to look good and feel polished. Dislikes: clutter and paragraphs of text on screens, anything that looks "AI generated", primitive-looking models ("spheres and cylinders"), pastel colours where they asked for bold.
- Gives quick, informal feedback mid-task; act on it, keep replies short, show screenshots of what changed.
- "Unlock all" (Settings) now defaults OFF: the owner is testing real progression. They found unlocks too fast, so milestones were made ~3× harder; neon needs finished shows.
- Name is **Globe Riders** (renamed from "Globe of Death" so it suits all ages / Play Families). The package `com.denbro.globeofdeath.app`, repo name and Expo slug keep the old name and must not change (Play upload key is tied to that package).

## Game overview
**Modes**
- **The show** (main): moves scroll right-to-left along a top "conveyor" track and must be hit as they reach the circle. 10 acts + finale/encore. Act lengths vary (16, 18, 20… bars, max 36). Between acts: pick 1 of 3 perks (`PERKS` in game/perks.js: Locked NOS, Double NOS, Hot NOS, Shield (3 free misses, ◆ pips by Balance; cards show shields left), Combo saver, Wide window (rest of the show), Double balance (the bar itself grows to double length; the extra right half is dark green `#balX` and wears away first, never refilled), Big tank (`game.balMax` 200: bar double length, extra half starts empty and fills in normal green; dashed border), Any way (next act: any direction swipe counts for any direction move), Slow-mo / Showtime ×2 (these two last one act, `game.actFx`), Thick skin, Showman, Nitro tank, Second wind; stored perks Pit stop / Spare NOS (6 s NOS) / Tea break (pause, then 3-2-1 back in with +20 balance) / Rewind (back to just before the 5th-last judged move, not past the act start: score, combo, balance, NOS charge and stats restored from `game.snaps` (taken in `snap()` as each move is judged), moves re-armed, tempo map re-anchored, then 3-2-1; judged notes now stay in the list 24 beats so they can come back) go in ONE action slot `game.stash` (gold pill under the track), fired by a QUICK swipe down (<260 ms flick, `useStash`; holding down is still a wheelie); picking a different stored perk asks Replace / Keep). Perk/curse picks use a fair Fisher–Yates `shuffle`. Curses: each show gets 0 (25%), 1 (50%) or 2 (25%) curses on random acts 3–10 (`planCurses` → `game.curseActs`); on a curse act the red "Pick your curse" card replaces the perk card — never both (`CURSES`): pick 1 of 3 (always one score curse among them), it lasts that act only (red pill under the track). Ride curses double points; score curses don't: Pickpocket (points ×0.1), Cold crowd (no combo bonus), Taxman (−20% score at act end), Tough judges (perfects count as Good), and instant ones applied on pick: Fine (−10,000 points), Combo breaker (combo to 0), Half marks (perfect count halved). Surviving a curse counts toward `game.cursesSurvived`, shown as a red skull ×N on show/combo score rows. The Lesson's last step shows a perk card then a curse card (nothing applied). Ride curses: Mirror (left/right swapped in press/release), Wheelie mania (a rule, not extra notes: in the gaps you must hold a wheelie — 0.6 beat either side of each move is free — otherwise ~10 balance/s drains with a WHEELIE! warning), Blind spot (moves fade before the circle), Fast forward (+15 BPM), Glass bones (×2 miss cost), No NOS, Decoys (red crossed-out moves — hitting one costs balance; `n.decoy`, no path turn), Lights out (`#dark` overlay), Dizzy (camera roll), Double time (patterns from 3 acts later), Mystery moves (notes are blank grey circles until 1 s before the press, `.note.blank`), Reverse (bike turned 180° riding rear-first, wheels spin back; track mirrored: `.track.rev`, circle on the right, moves travel left→right). `endActFx()` clears them at each act change. The next act's bars are only generated once its picks are made (loop.js gate), because curses/perks change its tempo (`actBpm`) and chart. `window.__curse = 1` forces the curse card (tests). Pausing (perk/curse/ad/pause) holds the exact beat (`game.heldB`) and `freeClock` re-anchors to it; `frame()` also takes out any lead the audio clock gains over `performance.now()` (>0.08 s within 2 s) so a ride never fast-forwards after resuming. A lost WebGL context (Android background) quits the ride (fans kept) and reloads the page when visible (`watchGL` in platform/app.js); the app reloads the WebView on `onRenderProcessGone`. Balance bar = health; misses cost balance, hits restore it; empty = crash. Difficulty ramps gently (act 1: 1 move per bar … act 9–10: 4–5 with half-beats). BPM rises +3 per act (96 → 130 max). Act N has N−1 other riders, always on the wall riding their own great circles (`idlePath`/`riderPos` in loop.js); when a jump is due, the free rider already nearest the crossing point (and crossing at a decent angle) gets their loop nudged by the smallest rotation that puts them there on the beat (gentle drift, no U-turns), then carries on — they never vanish; they never get knocked off: on a missed jump the rider hops clear (`dodgeB`) and you just lose balance. A coloured line projects ahead of a crossing rider (no head beacon / dots). Rider order is shuffled every run. Names (objects.js `CROSS`): Dario, Tess, Viktor, Lupe, Mack, Nico, Rosa, Bruno, Ines.
- **Free ride**: analog thumb stick (steer + throttle, speed persists), wheelies/stoppies, dodge riders, too slow over the top = fall. Survive as long as possible. Riders join at 10 s (a no-points warm-up: points only start when the first ghost arrives); all free-ride points are multiplied by riders + 1 (×2 with one rider…), shown gold next to Score (`#mult`, hidden at ×1); score rows show riders, not near misses, then +30, +40…; "<name> incoming!" 3 s before, then they ride as a 50%-opacity ghost (no collision) for 5 s.
- **Tutorial** ("Lesson" on the home screen): 11 steps, each must be performed before the next (left, right, camera button ×3, diagonals, jump, wheelie, stoppie, seat stand, steer during wheelie, NOS, action (quick swipe down uses a stored Pit stop), perks). The yellow act line only appears in the last step; when it reaches the circle a perk pick pops up. No crash possible.

**Controls (swipe anywhere, multi-touch)**
- Left/right swipe = turn; diagonals: up-left/up-right = gentle veer, down-left/down-right = hard turn. Turns are real heading changes on the sphere: `game/path.js` integrates a body-frame rate (π/2 round the loop + heading rate), each turn split into 12 constant-curvature arcs over one beat, then dead straight round the new great circle.
- Pull back (swipe down) and hold = wheelie; push forward and hold = stoppie; flick forward and release = jump (210 ms pending decision between jump and stoppie; skipped — stoppie fires instantly — when a stoppie note is due and no jump is near).
- Hold a side swipe = seat stand.
- During a held wheelie/stoppie, sliding the same thumb sideways ~16 px fires a turn (soft press: ignored if no matching note; 2× window). A second finger also works (14 px swipe, 1.5× window). While holding, any left-ish swipe counts for any left move (left/ul/dl), same for right.
- NOS button on the handlebars: when charged (needs full balance; charge rate 0.4 × hit value, made 25% harder at owner's request; any miss or wrong button empties it), next moves auto-play, with an afterburner flame out of the back of the bike (`makeNosFlame` in loop.js), faster wheel spin and a bigger FOV kick (the bike's speed itself stays locked to the beat); the blue NOS band on the track extends to the end of any long press it overlaps, and over any single move within 1 beat after its end (`nosEnd` in boost.js), so there's always a gap after NOS.
- Long-press notes (wheelie/stoppie/seat stand) are 2–6 beats; holds only end when the finger lifts. No single move within 1 beat either side of a long press end (owner found them ambiguous).

- Bikes: procedural "v2" builder (BSPEC per style): continuous frames, distinct engines (twin/single/V-twin/MX/four/jet), spoked or cast wheels, drilled discs, sprockets, chain, shocks with coil springs, exhausts, controls/cables/mirrors/dials, shaped tanks/seats/fairings (extruded side profiles), lights. Static parts merged per material for performance (`mergeGroup`).
- Bodywork pass (premium look): `loft(sections, mat)` in makeBike skins smooth moulded panels through cross-sections ({z,y,w,h,hb,n,tw}, Catmull-Rom, pointed ends) and `plate()` cuts thin fins/winglets. Sport = one continuous fairing (nose → belly pan), lofted tank, double-bubble screen, tail cowl, vents, winglets, stalk mirrors. Rocket = jet bike: needle-nose fuselage, canopy, reactor block with glowing core windows, neon shoulder strips, LED nose lights, canards, canted tail fins + spine fin, twin turbines either side of the rear wheel (intake fans, heat-blued nozzle petals, glowing cores; nozzle glow sprites from `S._noz`). Tyres are lathe-turned sections (flatter crown, wider rear on sport/rocket, sidewall ring, paint-colour rim stripe). Rider shoulder armour is fitted (suit colour + one stripe) instead of white caps.
- Rider: procedural "kit v3" (`riderKit`) — sculpted limbs/torso (`sculptGeo` with RGB vertex-colour panels), one-piece colour-blocked race leathers modelled on `art/rider-refs/` (suit material is white; each panel carries main / SEC white-grey stripe / BLK black via codes MAIN, SEC, BLK, DIM mapped through `M.pal`; helmet graphics via `M.hpal`). Leathers default suit colour = bike paint, armour sliders, aero hump, gauntlets wrapped on grips, tall race boots, race helmet (deformed sphere, big tinted visor). Posed with 2-bone IK to grips/pegs. The chest/back chevron on plain leathers is drawn per pixel (`sharpChevron`: torso carries a `chev` vertex attribute (lat, t, fr), kept through `mergeGroup`) so its edges are crisp. The helmet's centre stripe and side swoosh are drawn the same way (`sharpHelmet`; the shell carries its coordinates in `chev` with w = 2); costume helmets with their own `helmet` design stay vertex-coloured. The owner wants this pushed further toward their reference images (Dainese/Alpinestars-style race suits) but stylised, not hyper-real.
- Costumes (`rider/costumes.js`): each outfit has its own palette, a per-vertex suit design (`design(part, code, t, n, p, X, Y, Z)`; parts are tagged via `M.part` in `riderKit`), helmet graphics, sculpted props (stars + buckle, ruff/pom-poms/hair/nose, studs/mohawk/chain, shield + bolt, curved horns + spade tail, ermine collar + jewelled crown + medallion) and a shaped cape with lining (`makeCape`). Owner asked for detailed costumes, not primitives.
- Finishes: canvas-generated seamless textures applied triplanar (no UV stretching). Some move: `FIN_META.flow` drifts the pattern over time (lightning, lava, neon grid, galaxy, hologram); flames stay put and flicker/lick like fire (`fire`) and lightning's glow flickers (`flick`). Neon = white-hot core + saturated rim + pulsing additive halo shells (`neonify`).
- Studio env-map reflections on bikes/riders (`envify`). Spotlight shadows are OFF (they caused crawling cage-shadow flicker on floors). Logarithmic depth buffer on.
- First-person handlebars HUD at the bottom (DOM/SVG): bars steer and lift with stunts, tacho + NOS button (the orange throttle-marker line on the grip was removed at the owner's request).

**Audio**: WebAudio, all synthesised. `audio/synth.js`: voices -> music/sfx/ambience buses -> reverb (generated IR per arena) + tempo echo sends -> compressor -> master (mute) -> muffle LP. `audio/themes.js`: per-arena sound world (lead/stab/hit/jump voices, bass, kick/snare flavour, chord comping, pad, room, echo, no continuous noise beds (they read as static) — only one-off events like gulls/sirens/bubbles/radio beeps). Music bus = 1 in a ride, 0.33 in menus; crowd-cheer noise bursts (after wheelies/jumps/combos) play at 33%; `menuMusic()` loops the arena song in menus; audio starts on the first touch anywhere. `sfx(fn)` routes hit sounds/crashes to the sfx bus. Drum machine + bass + lead scheduled on the beat; tempo map supports pausing by shifting tempo times (`holdClock`/`freeClock`); `start()` must clear `game.heldAt` and `game.lastB`. The show clock is guarded in `frame()`: if the beat ever goes backwards (>2) or leaps ahead (>16) mid-ride, the tempo map is re-anchored to carry on from the last beat (phones threw rides back to the warm-up circle after app switches). `lat()` is capped at 0.3 s and frozen per ride (`fixLatency()` in start: the live value wobbled on phones). If the AudioContext claims 'running' but its currentTime stops moving for 0.6 s mid-ride, `rebuildAudio()` makes a fresh context and the guard re-anchors tempos, NOS end and hop timers (Android froze the clock with Bluetooth: free ride time stalled, shows stuck at the start). Tunes: songs can carry `mel` (8 bars × 8 eighth-notes of MIDI, 0 = hold, null = rest, top note ~F5) played on the theme's `mel` voice — the premium arenas and Big Top's new main song 'Big Top Bounce' (SONGS 18) are bouncy major-key kart-racer tunes the owner asked for (Diddy Kong Racing / Mario as reference). Hit sounds (owner approved, back to the original style): ONE note per press in the arena's hit voice, built on the current chord (`songRoot`) so moves form a tune with the music; each move its own pentatonic step (`NOTE`: down-left 0, left 1, up-left 2, right 3, up-right 4, down-right 5); the same move twice in a row alternates up one octave (never higher). Jump = quick rising chord flourish (no pitch-sweep boing). Wheelie/stoppie walk up/down the scale per beat; seat stand plays two notes. NOS = rising power-up arpeggio (`nosSound`). Ride music at 0.85 (menus 0.3), no ducking. Beach music × 0.75 with pan hits; Stadium uses Big Top's calliope for move sounds (owner hated the brass). Sky crane / Underwater / Moon move sounds were inaudible (soft metal, low bubble under the muffle, slow theremin) → punchy themed voices `pluck` / `drop` / `blip` with theme `sfx` 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.25. Owner hated: bells, high-pitched sounds, multi-note motifs, a walking melody, the jump boing and the old sawtooth NOS 'fart'. The game clock is the AudioContext clock — it's resumed on every touch and when returning to the app. If it's still suspended ~0.4 s into a running ride, `frame()` shows the pause menu so the Resume tap (a real gesture) restarts it (Android sometimes refuses to resume audio after another app). The NOS glow light stays in the scene at intensity 0 (toggling a light's visibility recompiles every material = stutter). Turn knots rebuild incrementally from the new turn's beat (`rebakeTurns(from)` in path.js).

## Code map (`src/`)
- `main.js` — boot: calls every module's `initX()` in the original order, then starts the frame loop. Modules only declare things at load time; anything that touches the scene, DOM or storage runs inside `initX()`, so import order never matters.
- `core/` — `constants.js` (R, GY, D, clamp, `$`…), `state.js` (`S`: mutable state shared across systems — score, combo, balance, act, run flags, input state…).
- `render/` — renderer (scene, camera, PMREM env), textures, lights, envmap (`envify`), neon (`neonify`), trails & rings, particles, camera (modes, orbit, resize).
- `arenas/` — stage (Big Top tent), crowd (sprite atlas → InstancedMesh), globe (cage), arenas (`ARENAS` table: env, crowd, build), current (`setArena`, `arenaTick`), life (`LIFE`), people (beach sprite people + extras), sealife (fish, sharks).
- `bikes/` — bike (`BSPEC`, `makeBike`, `mergeGroup`), finishes (`FIN_GEN`, `applyFinish`, triplanar), catalog (`CATALOG`), stats.
- `rider/` — rider (`sculptGeo`, `helmetDeform`, `riderKit`, `riderMats`, `buildRider`, `makeFigure`), ik (`ik2`), riderModel (unused Mixamo loader path — can delete or reuse for an imported model).
- `audio/` — synth (WebAudio voices/kits), clock (tempo map, `now`, `beatAt`, `timeAt`, acts), songs (`SONGS`, hit sounds, step scheduler).
- `game/` — path (sphere path: `posAt`/`qAt`/turns), freeride, objects (player, other riders), chart (`genBarNotes`, notes DOM), judging (`press`/`release`/`miss`), boost (NOS), perks, progress (arena unlocks), save (garage save), run (`start`, pause, quit), loop (`frame`).
- `input/` — keys, controls (swipe gestures + handlebar HUD).
- `ui/` — hud (judge/toast/balance), menus (home, levels, settings), garage (screen + 3D showroom), leaderboard, tutorial.
- `platform/ads.js` — test-ad placeholder. `platform/app.js` — bridge to the Expo app (window.ReactNativeWebView); AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads on the native side) will hook in here.
- `assets/crowd-atlas.png` — crowd sprites (byte-identical to the old embedded base64). Source sheets live in `art/`.
`window.__god` is a debug hook (press, release, state, fillBoost, useBoost, timeAt, now) used by headless tests.

## Testing approach that worked
- `npm test` (`tests/smoke.mjs`): perfect-timing bot, idle bot and free ride against the dev server.
- `tests/parity.mjs urlA urlB outDir` + `python3 tests/imgdiff.py outDir`: screenshots two builds with seeded `Math.random` and a frozen clock and diffs pixels (used to prove the module split changed nothing). Serve `original/` with a local `three.min.js`; it has no charset tag, so "·"/"…" show garbled there — expected. `ONLY=ride,menu` limits scenes.
- `tests/rider-shot.mjs out.png [style]` and `tests/outfits-shot.mjs out.png`: close-up contact sheets of bike+rider and of every outfit/finish (set `URL=` for another dev server).
- Headless Chromium (Playwright) with SwiftShader; serve the folder over `python3 -m http.server` (file:// blocks loading extra files). Replace the three.js CDN URL with a local `three.min.js` (r128) for offline tests.
- Synthetic `PointerEvent`s dispatched on `#touch` for gesture tests; `__god.press()` for timing bots. SwiftShader renders ~2 fps but the audio clock keeps real time.
- Render bikes/riders standalone with a custom camera for close-up checks.
